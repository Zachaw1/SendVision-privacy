Send Vision - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

Send Vision does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. All users are anonymous.

Analytics

Send Vision uses Amplitude, a third-party analytics service, to understand how the app is used and to improve the experience. All analytics data is anonymous and is not linked to your identity.

The following data is collected automatically by the Amplitude SDK:

  • A randomly generated device identifier (not your Apple ID or any personal identifier)
  • Device type and operating system version
  • Language and carrier
  • Coarse location (city, region, and country — derived from your IP address by Amplitude; your IP address is not stored)
  • App usage data such as session count, usage time, and feature interactions

No names, email addresses, accounts, or other personally identifiable information is collected. You cannot be identified from the data Send Vision collects.

Analytics data is transmitted to Amplitude's servers. For details on how Amplitude handles data, see the Amplitude Privacy Policy.

Camera & Microphone

Send Vision uses the device camera and microphone solely to record climbing attempt videos while the app is in use.

All video processing is performed on-device.

Photos Access

If enabled by the user, Send Vision may save recorded videos to the Photos library. Photos access is optional and can be disabled at any time.

On-Device Processing

All video recording, climbing detection, and ML processing is performed entirely on your device. Only anonymous analytics data is transmitted over the network.
