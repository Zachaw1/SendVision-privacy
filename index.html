Send Vision - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Send Vision does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

Data Collection

  • Send Vision does not collect analytics
  • Send Vision does not track users
  • Send Vision does not use third-party SDKs for data collection
  • Send Vision does not transmit data to any servers

Camera & Microphone

Send Vision uses the device camera and microphone solely to record climbing attempt videos while the app is in use.

All video processing is performed on-device.

Photos Access

If enabled by the user, Send Vision may save recorded videos to the Photos library. Photos access is optional and can be disabled at any time.

Offline Operation

Send Vision works entirely offline. No network connection is required.
